2. Personal data we may process

Depending on your relationship with Sindry, we may process the following categories of data:

Identification and account data: name or display name, email address, language, basic preferences, and internal authentication identifiers.

Business and membership data: Business name, the User's relationship to the Business, roles, billing country, verification status, plan, billing frequency, Membership status, validity dates, and Founder number when applicable.

Limited payment data: customer, subscription, price, session, invoice, or payment identifiers; status, amount, currency, billing country, and limited information provided by Stripe for reconciliation or fraud prevention. Sindry does not intend to store full card numbers or CVC codes.

Technical and security data: IP address, access date and time, authentication and security logs, device information, browser, operating system, or user-agent information generated by the infrastructure to operate and protect the service.

Support data: the content of messages, files, logs, or screenshots that the User chooses to share to resolve an issue.

3. Sensitive personal data

Sindry does not ordinarily request sensitive personal data to create an account or purchase a Membership. Users should not send sensitive information through support channels unless it is strictly necessary and expressly requested.

4. Operational Data stored locally

In the current desktop version, the ERP's primary Operational Data —for example products, supplies, customers, vendors, purchases, production, inventory, sales, collections, labels, and reports— is stored mainly in the User's local SQLite database and in the backups configured by the User.

Sindry does not upload the local operational database to its servers by default for analytics or telemetry. Authentication, Membership, billing, and security do use online services.

If Sindry later introduces cloud synchronization, a mobile app, online collaboration, additional telemetry, or another feature that processes Operational Data on servers, this Notice must be updated and the applicable acceptance or consent will be requested before such processing is enabled.

5. Necessary purposes

Create, authenticate, and manage the User account.

Create and manage the User's relationship with one or more Businesses, roles, and permissions.

Determine and validate plans, regional prices, the Founder promotion, and access rights.

Process, reconcile, and verify payments, renewals, cancellations, refunds, and billing through Stripe or other disclosed providers.

Detect country inconsistencies, fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, and security risks.

Provide support, diagnose failures, and respond to requests.

Send transactional, security, material-change, expiration, and automatic-renewal notices.

Comply with legal and contractual obligations and valid requests from authorities.

6. Secondary purposes and marketing communications

Sindry may send communications about news, features, surveys, or promotions only when there is an appropriate legal basis and, when required, consent. Marketing subscriptions will be optional and will not be preselected.

The User may object or withdraw consent by using the unsubscribe mechanism included in each communication or by writing to sindryerp@gmail.com. Refusing secondary purposes will not prevent access to essential contracted features.

7. Anonymized and aggregated information

Sindry may use information that has been anonymized or aggregated so that it cannot reasonably identify an individual for metrics, security, internal research, and product improvement.

The launch version does not use this permission as authorization to copy or generally analyze the ERP's local database. If a future feature involves additional telemetry collection or use of Operational Data, users will be informed in advance and the appropriate legal bases and consent mechanisms will be applied.

8. Providers, processors, and transfers

To operate Sindry, we may use technology providers that process information on behalf of the Data Controller, including authentication and database services such as Supabase, payment processing such as Stripe, email services, and web infrastructure providers such as Cloudflare. Some of these providers may operate infrastructure outside Mexico.

Disclosures or transfers will be limited to what is necessary to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, prevent fraud, or protect rights. When a transfer requires consent under applicable law, consent will be requested through the appropriate mechanism.

The User may request additional information about relevant providers by writing to sindryerp@gmail.com.

9. Retention and deletion

Data will be retained for as long as necessary for the stated purposes, the contractual relationship, and applicable legal, security, and legal-defense periods.

When data is no longer necessary, it will be blocked, anonymized, or deleted in accordance with applicable law. Deleting an account does not automatically delete information that must be retained by law or temporary copies that remain in reasonable provider backup cycles.

10. Security

Sindry will adopt reasonable administrative, technical, and physical measures to protect personal data under its responsibility against damage, loss, alteration, destruction, and unauthorized access or processing, taking into account the risks and available technology.

If a relevant security breach significantly affects the rights of data subjects, the notifications required by applicable law will be made.

11. ARCO rights and withdrawal of consent

The data subject may request Access, Rectification, Cancellation, or Objection (ARCO rights), and may withdraw consent when applicable, by sending a request to sindryerp@gmail.com.

The request must include sufficient information to identify the data subject, the right to be exercised, the data involved, and a means of receiving a response. Sindry may request additional information strictly necessary to verify identity or representation.

Requests will be handled within the time limits established by applicable law. Withdrawal of consent will not have retroactive effects and will not prevent processing that must continue because of a legal obligation or a legal basis other than consent.

12. Limitation of use or disclosure

The User may request limitations on promotional communications or non-essential processing by writing to sindryerp@gmail.com. Unsubscribe mechanisms included in marketing messages may also be used when available.

13. Websites, cookies, and tracking technologies

Sindry's confirmation, payment, and legal-information pages may be hosted through web infrastructure providers. The website uses the IP address to estimate country and display regional prices. This estimate does not by itself determine the billing country, which is validated when purchasing. The language preference is stored in the browser's local storage and may be deleted through browser settings. The initial version does not include Sindry-owned advertising cookies or advertising analytics tools.

If non-essential cookies, analytics, advertising, or other tracking technologies are added in the future, users will be informed and consent will be requested when necessary before use.

14. Changes to this Privacy Notice

Changes will be communicated through https://sindry-website.sindryerp.workers.dev/legal-en.html, within Sindry, by email, or through another reasonable method. When a change involves a new purpose that requires consent, consent will be requested before the corresponding processing occurs.

15. Applicable law and authority

This Notice is interpreted in accordance with applicable Mexican personal-data-protection law. The use of Sindry's internal mechanisms does not limit the data subject's right to approach the competent authority when applicable.
