This Policy explains the financial conditions of Sindry Memberships. It must be available before the User is sent to Checkout and supplements the Terms and Conditions.

1. Information before charging

Before confirming a purchase, Sindry will clearly display the selected plan, price, currency, billing frequency, recurring nature of the charge, the date or determinable rule for the first charge and later renewals, and any relevant Founder benefit or discount. For an immediate purchase, the first charge occurs when payment is completed and renewal is scheduled for the corresponding monthly or annual anniversary date, unless Stripe displays a valid adjustment to the User.

The User must provide express and informed consent to recurring charges. Acceptance must not be preselected or presented in a misleading manner.

2. Processing through Stripe

Stripe processes card data and charges. Sindry receives limited information about the payment result, subscription, and billing that is necessary to enable the service. Sindry does not store the full card number or CVC.

The status displayed by Sindry may take a short time to synchronize after a payment, cancellation, or payment-method update due to event processing between systems.

3. Automatic renewal and advance notice

Recurring Memberships will automatically renew according to the selected billing frequency unless canceled and as long as the payment method can be charged.

Sindry will send an automatic-renewal notice to the registered email address at least five calendar days before the next recurring charge. The notice will endeavor to clearly state the upcoming renewal and how to cancel.

When the User's applicable law establishes additional rights or notices, those requirements will prevail to the mandatory extent.

4. Cancellation

The User may cancel renewal through Stripe's portal or another accessible mechanism enabled by Sindry. Cancellation does not impose a penalty for the act of canceling.

Unless law, a refund, fraud, or another specific circumstance requires immediate termination, cancellation stops future charges and keeps paid features available through the final date of the current period.

At the end of the paid period, Sindry switches to the read-only mode described in the Terms: permanent read-only access to local information and available essential reporting, export, and backup functions.

5. General refund policy

Sindry does not offer a general voluntary additional 3- or 7-day refund period. Unless applicable law grants a refund right, there is a duplicate or erroneous charge, the issue is attributable to Sindry, or Sindry expressly approves an exception, charges for a period that has already started are considered earned and are not automatically refundable.

Non-use, change of mind, forgetting to cancel, insufficient storage space, failure to meet previously published technical requirements, incorrect configuration, data loss caused by lack of backups, or failures of Third-Party Services do not by themselves create a contractual right to a refund, without prejudice to mandatory legal rights.

Exceptional requests will be evaluated in good faith considering the timing of the charge, the cause, usage, the possibility of correcting the issue, and applicable law.

6. Duplicate charges, errors, and unauthorized payments

If Sindry or Stripe confirms a duplicate charge or a system error, the corresponding amount will be corrected or refunded. The User should report it as soon as reasonably possible.

Unauthorized payments will be investigated with Stripe and, when applicable, the issuer. Sindry may temporarily suspend the related Membership to prevent further charges or fraudulent use while the matter is investigated.

7. Country verification and refund for billing incompatibility

Sindry uses the Business's actual billing country to determine regional pricing and prevent abuse. The declared country must match the billing information validated by Stripe under Sindry's current logic.

If a material country discrepancy that invalidates the purchase is detected after a charge, Sindry may suspend and cancel the subscription and, when the system permits and it is appropriate, automatically initiate a refund of the rejected charge. The User must correct the actual country before purchasing again.

A card's issuing country may differ from the actual billing country; that fact alone does not constitute a violation.

8. Failed payments and retries

If a recurring payment fails, Stripe may retry the charge or request an updated payment method. Sindry may temporarily maintain normal access during a recovery period or display a past-due payment status, depending on the current configuration.

If payment is not brought current, the Membership may be suspended and eventually ended. The User will retain the read-access rights to Operational Data described in the Terms.

9. Chargebacks and bank disputes

The User retains legitimate rights to dispute unauthorized or incorrect charges. A chargeback may cause temporary suspension of the Membership while Stripe and financial institutions investigate.

Filing a chargeback does not by itself cause automatic permanent termination. If fraud, deliberate abuse of the chargeback process, use of an unauthorized card, or intentional denial of a valid charge accompanied by use of the service is confirmed, Sindry may terminate the Membership and take preventive measures in accordance with applicable law.

10. Founder price

The launch Founder promotion is limited to the first 300 eligible Businesses. The applicable Founder price and assigned number will be shown before or after payment according to the activation flow.

Founder status is associated with the Business and is retained if the same Business cancels and later reactivates its Membership. If the Business is voluntarily deleted, Founder status is lost, except when restored because of an error attributable to Sindry.

A change in ownership of the Business does not automatically transfer Founder status and may require Sindry's approval.

Founder price protection applies to the corresponding base Founder Membership price and does not necessarily apply to taxes, bank fees, currency conversion, future additional services, or charges imposed by third parties.

11. Annual plan

The annual plan is charged in advance for the period shown at Checkout. If it includes a benefit equivalent to included months or a discount compared with the monthly plan, that benefit will be reflected in the annual amount shown before payment.

Canceling annual renewal prevents the next charge but does not automatically create a prorated refund for a period that has already started, except when required by law or expressly approved by Sindry.

12. Promotions and codes

Promotions may be subject to dates, eligibility, region, and specific conditions. Founder benefits may exclude combining other coupons when disclosed before payment.

Any promotion offered after the first 300 Founders will be a different promotion and will not modify previously assigned numbers or benefits.

13. Price changes

Standard prices may change for future periods. When a change affects a renewal and the law requires notice or consent, Sindry will provide the required notice or obtain the required consent before charging.

Founder prices will be governed by the specific promise shown to the Business, except for items expressly excluded from protection or changes required by law.

14. Displayed amount and taxes

Sindry will display the applicable amount and currency before charging. When applicable, Checkout may show taxes included or itemized according to the current configuration and applicable law. Sindry does not guarantee fees, exchange rates, or other charges that may be applied by banks, issuers, or other third parties.

15. Billing support

For questions, duplicate charges, refunds, or billing issues: cuentas.sindry@gmail.com. For general support: sindryerp@gmail.com. Business phone: +52 664 405 7719.

When requesting help, include the account email, Business name, approximate date, and a description of the issue. Do not send full card numbers or CVC codes.
